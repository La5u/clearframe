# Deferred data plan

Priority 2 is deliberately deferred until the Priority-1 pipeline has measured
precision and Chromium cost.

- MBIC: annotator disagreement and demographic sensitivity evaluation.
- BASIL: lexical versus informational bias; evaluation only for context beyond a phrase.
- GoEmotions: passage-level emotion evaluation, with Reddit-domain caveats.
- VUA Metaphor Corpus: metaphor research/evaluation subject to BNC rights.
- SemEval idiomaticity, hyperbole, and simile corpora.
- ChangeMyView and NewEthos: passage-level ethos, logos, and pathos.
- Rhetorical-figure corpora for anaphora, epiphora, chiasmus, and related structure.
- Library of Congress free-to-use material for a larger rights-safe propaganda benchmark.

Do not add these until source-specific licenses, importers, domain-separated test
splits, and runtime budgets are defined.
